Lasagna

Description:

Lasagna, also known by the plural form lasagne, is a type of pasta made in wide, flat sheets.
It originates in Italian cuisine, where it is served in a number of ways, but is most often
served as a casserole made by stacked layers of pasta, alternating with fillings that could
consist of meat, dairy, vegetables, and sauces.Typically, cooked pasta is assembled with the
aforementioned fillings, topped with grated cheese, and then baked in an oven (al forno).
Regional variations of this are found across Italy,along with Americanized versions worldwide.

Ingredients

    • 9 lasagna noodles (boil according to package instructions)
      1 pound ground beef or Italian sausage
      1 jar (24 oz) marinara or meat pasta sauce
      15 oz ricotta cheese (or cottage cheese)
      2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
      1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
      1 large egg (mixed into the ricotta)Instructions

    • Instructions:

    • step1: Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
      step2: Brown the ground beef in a pan, drain extra fat, and stir in the marinara sauce.
      Simmer for a few minutes.
      step3: Stir together the ricotta cheese, egg, and a pinch of salt and pepper in a small bowl.
      step4: Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in a 9x13-inch baking dish. Place 3 cooked noodles
      on top, spread with a portion of the ricotta mix, add meat sauce, and sprinkle
      with mozzarella. Repeat to make 3 layers. Finish with noodles, remaining sauce,
      and a heavy topping of mozzarella and Parmesa
      step5: Cover the dish with foil and bake for 30 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another
      15 minutes until bubbly and golden brown.
      step6: Let the lasagna rest for 15 minutes before slicing and serving.
