Chicken soup

Description:

For a powerful, therapeutic bowl that fights off congestion and inflammation, the Spicy Ginger Garlic Chicken Soup is an exceptional choice.
It combines the proven immune-boosting benefits of a traditional chicken broth with heavy doses of fresh ginger, garlic, and targeted
heat to open up your sinuses and soothe your throat.

Ingredients

    • 1.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts (cut into bite-sized pieces)
    • 6 cups high-quality chicken bone broth
    • 2 tbsp fresh ginger, grated or minced
    • 4 cloves garlic, minced
    • 1 tsp cayenne pepper (adjust to your heat tolerance)
    • 1 tsp ground turmeric
    • 1 jalapeño pepper, sliced (optional, for extra heat)
    • 1 tbsp olive oil or coconut oil
    • 1 medium onion, finely diced
    • 2 medium carrots, sliced
    • 2 stalks celery, chopped
    • 2 cups bok choy or baby spinach
    • Juice of 1 lemon or lime
    • Salt and freshly cracked black pepper to taste

    • Instructions:

    • step1: Heat the oil in a large soup pot over medium heat. Add the diced onion, minced garlic, and grated ginger.
      Sauté for 3 to 4 minutes until the mixture turns fragrant and soft.
      step2:Stir in the turmeric, cayenne pepper, and a pinch of black pepper. Stir constantly for about 1 minute to
      toast the spices and unlock their essential oils.
      step3:Pour in the chicken bone broth. Add the chopped chicken pieces, carrots, celery, and sliced jalapeño.
      Bring the liquid to a gentle boil.
      step4:Turn the heat down to low-medium, cover the pot, and let it simmer for 20 to 25 minutes.
      Ensure the chicken is fully cooked through and the vegetables are tender.
      step5:Uncover the pot and stir in your bok choy or baby spinach. Let it cook for 3 to 4 minutes until the greens wilt into a bright green color.
      step6:Turn off the heat and stir in the fresh lemon or lime juice. The vitamin C from the citrus balances out
      the rich broth and cuts through the intense heat. Season with extra salt if needed, ladle into bowls, and serve piping hot!
